The Work-in-Progress Poster Session presents early-stage, ongoing, or recently completed research and practice-driven work submitted through the [Call for Posters](/call_for_posters/). Poster presentations do not constitute an accepted paper and are not published in the ACM conference proceedings. This session is separate from the [Highlighted Poster Sessions](/program/poster_session/) associated with accepted papers.

*Saturday, November 7, 12:00–13:30 (with lunch), [Speerträger](https://www.lmu.de/raumfinder/#/building/bw0000/map?room=000001006_), LMU main building*
